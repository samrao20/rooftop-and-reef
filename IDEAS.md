# Quirky ideas

Everything here works on a plain static site (no server), because each of us only writes to our own card file. Effort: S = an evening, M = a weekend, L = a bigger build.

## Make it feel alive
1. **The Seam** (M). The plaza is split in two: Townsville beach on the left, the Mumbai rooftop on the right, and you stand where they meet. Each half follows that city's real clock and live weather. When it is monsoon rain in Mumbai, it rains on his half. When it is 2am for him, his half is a sleeping rooftop with one lit window. You can *see* each other's day.
2. **The Overlap Hour** (S). Townsville and Mumbai are 4.5 hours apart. The portal works out when you are both awake and glows ("you are both up, say hi") with a little bridge of lights joining the two halves.
3. **Weather and tide as moods** (S). The sea gets choppy on the days your week score is low, calm when high. Pure decoration, never a judgement.
4. **Footprints and lit windows** (S). When someone publishes, their character leaves footprints to the notice board. A window on the skyline lights for each quest they cleared that week.

## Things you can do to each other (all friendly, none can hurt)
5. **Lantern post** (M). Send one line, written on your own card, that shows up as a paper lantern drifting across the other person's half. "Saw this and thought of you." Opens when clicked. Lanterns fade after a week.
6. **Shadow exchange** (M). Once a week your highest-tier shadow "visits" the other person's plaza and wanders around over there, wearing a little travel hat. It goes home on Sunday.
7. **Hat shop and gifting** (M). Clearing a gate unlocks a cosmetic (a dabba, a snorkel, a kite). You can gift one to the other: it appears on their character. Cosmetics only.
8. **Respect emotes** (S). Click a character to send a tiny emote (🙌, ☕, 🌊) stored on your own card. No messages, no pressure, just "I saw that".
9. **Pass the flame** (M). The diya on his roof and the campfire on your beach are linked. When you both clear 3 quests in the same day they glow together, violet when you both finish all five.

## Games (small, silly, async)
10. **Kite Duel** (M). A tiny one-button Mumbai kite-fighting game. Best score goes on your card, and the loser owes the winner a chai (virtually).
11. **Reef Dash or Fishing** (M). A calm reef fishing game; catch real reef fish that unlock in the plaza aquarium.
12. **Weekly bounty board** (S). A pointless-prize head-to-head each week, picked by a coin flip: most pages read, most steps, first to clear a gate, earliest night log. Winner gets a silly title for seven days ("Keeper of the Chai").
13. **Co-op gate** (M). A shared gate with six stages. You clear three, he clears three. It only opens when both halves are done.

## Accountability without guilt
14. **Sunday promise** (S). Each Sunday each of you posts one promise for the week on your own card. Next Sunday it shows on the board and you each mark it yourself: done, partly, or "life happened". No scoring.
15. **Help board with thanks** (S). When someone solves an ask, the asker marks it done and adds a thank-you; the helper gains a "Helper" rank that only ever goes up.
16. **Swap a rabbit hole** (S). Each week the plaza picks one topic from each of your topic libraries and shows it to the other person.

## Easter eggs
17. **Type `chai`** and it rains tea. **Type `reef`** and the plaza goes underwater for ten seconds. The Konami code triggers a Sea Link light show.
18. **Time capsule** (S). Write a note that opens on a date you pick. Remember it is in the repo, so it is for fun, not secrets.

## Suggested first slice
The Seam + click-for-card + the notice board (ideas 1, 5 and 14 stripped down). It looks good, it is useful from day one, and nothing in it needs more than the two cards.

## Rules for every idea
- Nothing can penalise anyone. Everything is a gift, a glow or a nudge.
- Each person only writes to their own card.
- Nothing private ever goes into a card. If an idea needs private data, we drop the idea.
